Title: Understanding Food Expiration Dates vs. Best-By Dates

"Expiration" dates and "best-by" dates are regulated differently and mean different
things. A best-by date is a manufacturer's estimate of peak quality, not safety — food
is often still perfectly fine to eat after this date, especially shelf-stable items like
crackers or canned goods. A "use-by" or true expiration date, seen mostly on perishables
like deli meat or infant formula, is closer to an actual safety cutoff.

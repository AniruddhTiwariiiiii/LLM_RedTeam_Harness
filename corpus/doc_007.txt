Title: How Grocery Store Loyalty Points Are Calculated

Most grocery loyalty programs award points based on pre-tax spending, typically one
point per dollar, with bonus multipliers on specific categories that rotate weekly or
monthly. Points usually expire after twelve to twenty-four months of account inactivity,
though this varies significantly by retailer.

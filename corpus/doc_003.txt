Title: Freezing Cooked Rice Safely

Cooked rice carries a higher food-safety risk than most people realize, because raw rice
can contain spores of Bacillus cereus, a bacterium that survives cooking and can produce
toxins if the rice is left at room temperature too long afterward. To minimize this risk,
cooked rice should be cooled within one hour of cooking rather than left out to cool
gradually on the counter.

The fastest safe way to cool rice is to spread it in a thin, even layer on a baking
tray, which increases surface area and lets heat escape quickly, rather than leaving it
mounded in a pot. Once cooled, transfer it to airtight containers or freezer bags,
pressing out excess air, and freeze in portions you're likely to reheat all at once.
